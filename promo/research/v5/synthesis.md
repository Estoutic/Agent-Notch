# Promo v5 — synthesis of six research reports (2026-10-14)

Sources: `1-claude-tooling.md`, `2-launch-films.md`, `3-3d-devices.md`, `4-motion-craft.md`, `5-sound.md`, `6-concept.md` in this folder (Codex gpt-6.1-sol high, every claim linked and dated).

## Why v4 is a 3/10 (all six agree)
- It is a feature catalogue: 24 sections, 13 features at the same scale, small dark panels in the same place, captions doing the work the picture should do.
- The strong opening ("12 agents. One bottleneck. You.") is spent; nothing escalates after it.
- The 3D laptop has an empty dark screen and is wide, so the notch — the product — is invisible. More render time on the chassis fixes nothing.
- The climax (the drop) lands on an empty laptop, not on the decision.
- The vertical cut is miniature UI between black areas.

## What a 10/10 needs (convergent)
1. **One story, not a tour.** blocked → spotted → answered → working → finished. Features that don't serve it go to separate short clips.
2. **Shorter master.** 30–45 s (launch-film report: 30–35; concept report: 55). Separate vertical cut authored for phones, not cropped.
3. **Real product pixels and real causality.** Native app UI (snapshot or screen recording), one real Allow → terminal resumes. No redrawn React facsimiles where the real app can be shown.
4. **The amber dot is the through-line.** Match cut: prompt dot → "You." period → notch mark → green. The notch fills the frame at least once.
5. **Climax on the decision.** Musical release on Allow, silence on "You.", descending cue on Deny, motif repeat on completion.
6. **Scale changes.** Alternate macro / panel / wide; 6–15-frame inserts for chaos, 45–90-frame holds for anything readable.
7. **Hardware with something on it.** Either a real MacBook photographed by the owner (Apple's third-party guidelines favour a photo of genuine hardware over an artist's render) with UI composited, or the Blender shot rebuilt around the notch with real UI on the glass.
8. **Process gates.** 3 still frames → animatic → owner approval → production → phone check muted and with sound.

## Concepts on the table
- A. "We replaced our slowest agent" — performance-review parody; reveal: the slowest agent is YOU. (concept report's pick)
- B. "You're the bottleneck" 2.0 — straight single-rescue film, 32–40 s. (launch-films report's pick)
- C. "Something stopped" — horror fake-out: tension, the monster is "May I run the tests?"
- D. "Approval League" — sports-broadcast parody with an announcer.
- E. "The waiting room" — agents as office workers waiting for the human (heaviest to produce).

## Tools worth adding (ranked)
- Remotion official skills (`npx skills add remotion-dev/skills`) — free, highest return.
- Real capture of the app (macOS screen recording free; Screen Studio $29/mo optional).
- ElevenLabs Starter $6/mo — custom SFX, optional deadpan voice.
- New Suno v6 track built around the new structure (owner generates).
- Blender: keep the script pipeline; official Blender Lab MCP only for inspection.
- Not worth it now: AE/Resolve/Spline purchases, AI-generated video of the laptop or UI, framework migration, Sora (API removed 2026-09-24).

## Risks
- Trademarks: no Claude/OpenAI logos as characters or jokes; independence line stays.
- No implied auto-approval; risky commands are always shown as a deliberate human decision.
- "20 minutes waiting" is a dramatisation; the app hands back to the agent's window after 100 s.
- Suno and stock licences: keep raw assets out of the MIT repo.
