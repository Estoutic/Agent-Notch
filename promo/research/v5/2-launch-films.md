# Agent Notch: launch-film research

Research: 13–14 October 2026. Publication dates appear beside sources; “checked 14 October 2026” means the primary page is undated. Editorial judgments and proposed timings are recommendations, not measured conversion results.

## The film to make

Twelve agents are moving. One stops. You keep working, unaware. Twenty minutes disappear. An amber mark beside the notch makes the interruption visible; one deliberate approval restarts the work.

That is the film. I recommend a 30–35-second launch cut built around this single rescue, with a separate walkthrough for the feature set. A “top-1” ranking or guaranteed 10/10 is UNVERIFIED and cannot be established by reference research.

I inspected both supplied v4 contact sheets, decoded a periodic frame montage from the actual MP4, and read its timeline, device compositor and Blender scripts. ffprobe reports 1920×1080, 30 fps, 59.648 seconds. The timeline contains 24 named sections. The opening has an argument: “12 agents,” “One bottleneck,” “You.” The middle spends that argument on successive demonstrations: states, counters, projects, usage, jumping, keys, questions, plans, cards, sounds, themes, presets and hosts.

My objection is the repeated visual scale. Small panels occupy similar positions on similar dark backgrounds. Captions carry information the picture should dramatize. The wide laptop reveal makes the distinguishing feature—the notch—almost invisible. The supplied reveal frame has a largely empty dark screen; spending more render time on its chassis will not repair that.

The existing pipeline is better than the film suggests: it already exports screen-corner coordinates and projects screen content into Blender frames. Populate and compose that screen before rebuilding the device.

## What makes the difference

My strongest conclusion is that premium motion preserves attention across changes of scale. A cursor, amber dot or notch edge becomes the next shot’s anchor. The audience understands why the camera moved. A generic zoom merely changes size.

Use a fast burst to establish overload, then a conspicuous pause. Give permission text a stable reading interval. Cut when the user understands the action; reserve musical accents for decisions and consequences. The proposed range is 6–15-frame inserts for chaos and 45–90-frame holds for important UI at 30 fps—not measured rules extracted from these brands.

A useful 32-second animatic:

| Time | Picture and purpose |
|---|---|
| 0–2 s | Twelve working sessions; one permission and “20:00 waiting” become the dominant detail. Establish the loss immediately. |
| 2–5 s | Everything arrests. “YOU.” One amber dot survives the silence. |
| 5–9 s | Match the dot into the real notch mark; reveal just enough MacBook to establish location. |
| 9–17 s | Native panel opens. Hold on the command; show one intentional Allow action. Preserve request → approval → resumed work. |
| 17–25 s | Widen to multiple projects moving again; briefly show the hotkey and a completion toast. |
| 25–32 s | “Keep them moving.” Agent Notch. Free / open source / local. Hold the install command. |

The waiting timer is a dramatized scenario, not a measured speedup. A risky-command example should visibly require a decision. Successful editing must not imply blanket automatic approval.

## Claude Code production stack, ranked by return per hour

All commands below are proposed for a later production session; none was installed or executed during this research.

**1. Existing Remotion 4.0.290 + native Swift captures.** Highest return. Keep the lockfile; there is no demonstrated need to upgrade. Claude edits shot timing and framing, uses the app’s actual snapshot renderer, and assembles those assets in React. Source: local `promo/package.json`, `Sources/AgentsNotch/Snapshot.swift`, and `scripts/build.sh`, inspected 14 October 2026. Future commands:

```sh
cd promo && npm ci
npx skills add remotion-dev/skills
```

The [official skills documentation, updated 13 October 2026](https://www.remotion.dev/docs/ai/skills) verifies that install command. Alternatively, the [official Claude plugin](https://www.remotion.dev/docs/ai/claude-code-plugin) documents:

```sh
claude plugin marketplace add remotion-dev/claude-code-plugin
claude plugin install remotion@remotion
```

For captures, from the repo root after building: `AGENT_NOTCH_LANG=en build/AgentsNotch.app/Contents/MacOS/AgentsNotch --snapshot promo/public/native`. This produces fixture screenshots, not proof of live interaction. Cost: Remotion is free for individuals/companies up to three people under its terms; larger collaborations have paid requirements. [Pricing, checked 14 October 2026](https://www.remotion.dev/docs/license/pricing).

**2. FFmpeg 9.0.1 and the existing audio script.** Version verified locally. This is supported by the local script; it was not run. Cost: no additional software purchase. Full rendering waits until composition works.

## What NOT to do

Do not put all thirteen themes, every settings panel and every interaction in the launch cut. Do not add another empty-screen laptop orbit, random particles, generic glitch transitions or compulsory camera movement. Do not redraw native UI merely to make animation easier. Do not let beat synchronization override comprehension.

Do not buy Cinema4D, an AI-video subscription or a new rendering pipeline before the animatic proves the story. Do not adopt generated teardown percentages or “viral” labels as evidence. Do not assume a familiar brand has one universal edit grammar: Raycast’s presenter opening and Cursor’s light launch film contradict that shortcut.
