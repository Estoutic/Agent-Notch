# Promo v4 — plan (owner-approved direction, 2026-10-13)

## Story
One task, one journey: blocked → spotted → answered → working → finished. Every feature fits into it.
Cold open "You're the bottleneck":
- 0–2 s: terminal cascade, "12 agents."
- 2–4 s: one permission prompt freezes, everything else dims; "One bottleneck."
- 4–5 s: black + silence; "You."
- 5–7 s: the prompt's amber dot match-cuts to an amber mark beside the notch (3D MacBook reveal).
- 7–10 s: click the notch, approve a safe command, output resumes; "Agent Notch. Keep them moving."
Fix from critique: never say "without your click" (⌃⌥Y approves too). Use "Only you approve."

## 60 s allocation (critique §3)
hook/reveal 10 · awareness 9 · permission/question/plan workflow 17 · inspection/completion 9 · customisation 8 · compatibility/local/install 7.

## Craft (critique §4 + research)
1. Edit: 12–24-frame connective cuts alternating with 60–90-frame reading holds; no duplicate claims.
2. Cursor/keys/agent states driven by one event timeline; every action shows its consequence.
3. Type: Geist (display) + Inter (body), OFL; whole-phrase entrances; fixed roles.
4. Sound: UI SFX from the Node synth placed on events; music ducks under them; master ≈ -14 LUFS.
5. Match cuts; @remotion/motion-blur only on fast travel; neutral blacks, amber = needs you, red = danger, green = done; less purple fog.
6. 3D MacBook rendered in Blender (procedural model, Poly Haven CC0 HDRI), screen corners exported per frame for live UI overlay.
7. Themes: one panel reskins in place, three hero looks, "13 themes."
8. Vertical: re-edited for phones, enlarged crops, no dead zones.
